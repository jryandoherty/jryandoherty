When Gerald Wiggins makes art, he is "not necessarily trying to say something to people. I'm just trying to make them happy, because there is not enough happiness."


Wiggins’ depictions of human figures, animal life, and fantastical creatures are stunningly accomplished. His drawings are uncluttered, precise and use a spare, controlled line and careful coloring to convey detail.
